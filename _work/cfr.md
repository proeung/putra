    <h2>Storybook Component Library</h2>

    <h3>Challenge:</h3>

    <p>One of the biggest challenges was building ahead of design. With the design team still finalizing specs, I had to lean on my own design background to make informed decisions: filling in gaps, maintaining visual consistency, and ensuring components were ready to accept final specs without major rework. All of this needed to move quickly within a tight timeline, with each component mapping cleanly to WordPress Gutenberg blocks for the editorial team.</p>

    <h3>Solution:</h3>

    <p>Working within Upstatement's existing Storybook setup, housed directly inside the Next.js project, I developed and organized components with TailwindCSS and React, structuring each story to mirror its corresponding Gutenberg block and serve as a clear reference for variants, props, and usage. To ensure design-to-code fidelity, we maintained a thorough PR review process where each component was spot-checked against design specs before merge, catching drift early and keeping quality high throughout.</p>

    <h3>Results:</h3>

    <p>The Storybook library became the team's primary reference throughout the engagement, enabling faster handoffs between design and development. By the end of the four-month engagement, components were production-ready and integrated directly into the CFR.org platform, immediately usable by editorial teams without developer intervention.</p>

## Summary of Role

- Contracted by Upstatement as a support engineer to accelerate UI development during a critical phase of the CFR.org redesign
- Built and organized production-ready React UI components with TailwindCSS within Upstatement's existing Storybook setup (housed inside the Next.js project), each mapped to WordPress Gutenberg blocks for content authoring
- Built ahead of finalized design specs, drawing on a strong design background to make informed decisions and maintain visual consistency while designs were still in progress
- Maintained a rigorous PR review process to ensure design-to-code parity, catching drift early before components reached production
- Collaborated closely with Upstatement's PM, designers, and two lead engineers throughout a focused four-month engagement
